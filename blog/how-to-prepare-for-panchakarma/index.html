How to prepare for Panchakarma

Good preparation makes Panchakarma smoother and safer. Most of it is simple: honest information for the doctor, a lighter routine and enough time set aside. This is how families in Patna usually plan for it.

Start with the consultation

Bring your prescriptions, recent blood reports and a list of any supplements. Tell the doctor about past surgeries, pregnancy or the possibility of it, and any heart, blood pressure or sugar concerns.

‍

Never stop a prescribed medicine on your own. Your plan is made around your medicines, together with the doctor who prescribed them.

Planning your days around the programme

1. Choose the right time

Seasons matter in Ayurveda. The doctor may suggest a particular time of year for a particular procedure, as part of ritucharya, the seasonal routine.

2. Clear your calendar

  • Avoid travel during the programme
  • Keep work light on main therapy days
  • Plan for early nights and rest
  • Arrange a family member for support if needed

3. Eat lighter in the days before

Warm, simple, home-cooked food is ideal. Cut down on fried food, outside meals, alcohol and very late dinners, unless advised otherwise.

Ask every question you have

You should know what will happen each day before it happens. If anything in your plan is unclear, ask at the consultation. A well-informed patient is part of what keeps Panchakarma safe.
